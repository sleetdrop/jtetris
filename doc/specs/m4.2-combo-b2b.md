# M4.2 Combo and Back-to-Back Scoring Spec

## Spec ID
`M4.2-COMBO-B2B`

## Status
`Done`

## Scope
- Add combo scoring for consecutive line-clear locks.
- Add back-to-back (B2B) chain tracking for difficult clears.
- Integrate baseline T-Spin line-clear scoring into score calculation.

## Out of scope
- T-Spin Mini rules.
- Score breakdown panel rendering (M4.3).
- Guideline-perfect scoring parity.

## Design notes
- Combo streak starts at 0 on the first clear and gives bonus from the second consecutive clear onward.
- B2B eligible events: Tetris and T-Spin line clears.
- B2B chain survives non-clearing locks and breaks on non-difficult line clears.
